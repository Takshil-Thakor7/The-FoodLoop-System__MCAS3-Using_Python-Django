Environmental Impact

Food waste in landfills produces methane, a greenhouse gas 80x more potent than CO2. Wasted food also wastes all the water, energy, and land used to produce it.

Economic Impact

Food waste costs the global economy over $1 trillion per year. Households throw away 30-40% of food they purchase. Restaurants waste 4-10% of food before it reaches customers.

Social Impact

While 828 million people face hunger, one-third of food produced globally is wasted. Redistributing just 25% of wasted food could feed every hungry person.

Take Action

Solutions to Reduce Food Waste

Simple steps everyone can take to make a massive impact on the global food waste crisis.

Plan Your Meals

Make shopping lists, plan weekly meals, and buy only what you need to avoid over-purchasing.

Proper Storage

Store food at correct temperatures, use airtight containers, and follow the First-In-First-Out rule.

Donate Surplus Food

Connect with local food banks, NGOs, and community kitchens through FoodLoop.

Compost Food Scraps

Turn unavoidable food waste like peels and cores into nutrient-rich compost for gardens.

Support Food Rescue

Volunteer with FoodLoop to help collect and deliver surplus food to those in need.

Spread Awareness

Educate your community about the impact of food waste and share simple everyday solutions.
